Four roles, one site record

Administrator

The business owner. Creates the team, invites everyone by email, and decides what each person does. Roles can be changed later as the crew shifts.

Supervisor

Runs the work. Assigns inspection tasks, turns findings into work orders, and tracks what is open, on hold, or done.

Field Inspector

Walks the site against an assigned task and files what they find, with geotagged photos and a severity rating.

Field Worker

Sees only the work assigned to them, in severity order, and posts progress and completion photos from a phone.

About us

Small construction teams rarely lose track of a problem because nobody noticed it. They lose track because the notice lived in a group chat, a camera roll, and somebody's memory of what the foreman said on Tuesday.

ConstructFlow is built around one idea: a finding is not useful until it has a severity, an owner, and a deadline attached to it. Every inspection filed in the system becomes a work order somebody is accountable for, and the most dangerous items rise to the top on their own.

It runs in a browser and installs to a phone, so a worker on scaffolding and an owner at a desk are reading the same record.
