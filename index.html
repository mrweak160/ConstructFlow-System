How it works

Four roles, one site record

Each role works from the same record, seeing what their part of the job requires.

01

Administrator

The business owner. Creates the team, invites members by email, and assigns each person their role. Roles may be reassigned at any time as the crew changes.

02

Supervisor

Directs the work. Assigns inspection tasks, converts findings into work orders, and monitors what remains open, on hold, or completed.

03

Field Inspector

Inspects the site against an assigned task and files each finding with geotagged photos and a severity rating.

04

Field Worker

Receives only the work assigned to them, ordered by severity, and submits progress and completion photos from a phone.

About us

A finding is not actionable until someone owns it

Small construction teams rarely lose track of a hazard because no one noticed it. They lose track because the record of it was scattered across a group chat, a camera roll, and someone's recollection of what the foreman said on Tuesday.

ConstructFlow is built on a single principle: a finding is not actionable until it carries a severity, an owner, and a deadline. Every inspection filed in the system becomes a work order for which someone is accountable, and the most hazardous items rise to the top on their own.

The system runs in any browser and installs to a phone, so a worker on scaffolding and an owner at a desk are working from the same record.

The workflow of a single finding
1
Task assignedA supervisor directs an inspector to a specified area of the site.
2
Inspection filedPhotos, location data, and a record of what was observed on site.
3
Severity ratedCritical, high, moderate, or low. That rating determines its position in the queue.
4
Work order issuedA named crew, a designated lead, and a deadline. No work is left unassigned.
5
Verified, then closedThe supervisor reviews the work and the inspector verifies it on site. Both are required before the order can close.
