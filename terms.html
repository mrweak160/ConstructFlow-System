2. What ConstructFlow Is

ConstructFlow is a web-based system for construction teams to record site inspections and coordinate the work that follows from them. It lets administrators manage who is on a team and what role they hold, lets supervisors assign inspection tasks and turn findings into work orders, lets field inspectors file reports with photos and a severity rating, and lets field workers see and complete the work assigned to them.
